ROUTINE / FACT
I go / I live / I work
Present Simple — rutyna, fakt, coś regularnego.

I go to the gym three times a week.
I live in Wakefield.
I work 12-hour shifts.

NOW / ARRANGEMENT
I am going / I am working
Present Continuous — teraz, tymczasowo lub zaplanowane.

I am going to the gym now.
I am working tonight.
I am meeting her tomorrow.

PAST
I went / I worked / I lived
Past Simple — zakończona czynność w przeszłości.

I went to the gym yesterday.
I worked last weekend.
I lived in Poland before I moved to the UK.

PAST HABIT
I used to go
Used to — kiedyś robiłem / byłem, ale teraz już niekoniecznie.

I used to go to the gym after work.
I used to live in Poland.
I used to be shy.

Com and Sup — Comparative & Superlative

Dzisiejsza lekcja. Comparative = stopień wyższy. Superlative = stopień najwyższy. Dodałem też taski, linking words i 15 słów z zeszytu.

Comparative — 2 rzeczy

cheap → cheaper than
small → smaller than
expensive → more expensive than
comfortable → more comfortable than

Superlative — jedna z grupy

cheap → the cheapest
small → the smallest
expensive → the most expensive
comfortable → the most comfortable

Spelling

easy → easier → the easiest
big → bigger → the biggest
large → larger → the largest
quiet → quieter → the quietest

Irregular

good → better → the best
bad → worse → the worst
little → less → the least
many/much → more → the most
old → older → the oldest

Task 2 — rental adverts

Property A: 1-bed flat, £650, small kitchen, near town centre, noisy.
Property B: 3-bed house, £950, large garden, village, quiet.

  1. A is cheaper than B.
  2. B is more spacious than A.
  3. A is closer to the town centre than B.
  4. B has the largest garden of all the properties.
  5. Comparing only A and B: B is the better option for a big family.

Better vs best: przy dwóch opcjach „the better option”; z większej grupy „the best option”.

Task 3 — However / Therefore / In addition

However = jednak / kontrast.
Detached houses are very quiet. However, they are expensive to buy.

Therefore = dlatego / skutek.
The student studied hard. Therefore, she passed her Entry 3 exam.

In addition = dodatkowo.
Renting a flat is flexible. In addition, it allows you to move easily.

Homework — odpowiedzi

clean → cleaner → the cleanest
modern → more modern → the most modern
easy → easier → the easiest
good → better → the best

Przykładowe 3 zdania:
My living room is larger than my kitchen.
My kitchen is brighter than my living room.
My living room is more comfortable than my kitchen.

Irregular adjectives — poprawki

good → better → the best
bad → worse → the worst
far → farther/further → the farthest/furthest
little (amount) → less → the least

old → older → the oldest
family: my elder brother / the eldest brother

Nie: less smaller than. Poprawnie: less than. Nie używaj zwykle elder than; powiedz my elder brother, ale my brother is older than me.
